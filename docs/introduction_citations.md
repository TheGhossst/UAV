Left uncited on purpose: nearest-UAV association, the per-link cap, the limited step around a frozen layout, the method paragraph's extra starts and controls, and the final contribution paragraph. The method-name paragraph names multi-start, zenith-anchor initialization, the medoid, random-subset, and scattered-layout controls, and particle swarm optimization. Zenith-anchor initialization is a start for the same approximation. Particle swarm optimization scores candidate layouts by an equal split of the pool while it searches, then the finished layout is scored with the bandwidth allocation used for the other methods. The final paragraph states this paper's findings from `docs/observations.md`: on the larger field an overhead start still raises the sum rate when a common rule gives every link the same share, and that gain is geometric; the smaller field sits near the best-link ceiling and the initializations nearly agree; the sum-rate solution meets the age threshold when the split is free and falls below it under that same share; arrival rate and processor speed move the objective less than placement. The per-link cap and the residual-bandwidth rule are stated once, in the program paragraph. No measured rates are written in the introduction.
